    About Rangam

    Woven by families who never left the loom.

    A Kanchipuram saree is built in two halves. The body and the border are woven separately and then interlocked by hand, which is why the border holds even after decades of wear. Rangam works with weaver households in Kanchipuram and sells their silk directly, with the weave and zari details printed on every tag.

    1. Mulberry silk is reeledFine silk thread is twisted into the strong yarn used for warp and weft.
    2. The yarn is dyedSkeins are dyed in small lots so each colour sits deep in the thread.
    3. The loom is warpedThousands of threads are set by hand before the first pass of the shuttle.
    4. Body, border and pallu are wovenZari motifs are added pick by pick, then the three parts are interlocked.
    5. Finishing and checksEach piece is inspected, labelled and packed in cotton.

    Questions

    Before you buy.

    How do I tell real Kanchipuram silk?

    Look for the Silk Mark label, a border that is interlocked with the body rather than stitched on, and zari that looks the same on both faces. Genuine pieces also feel heavy and drape with weight.

    How should I care for my saree?

    Dry clean only. Store it folded in a soft cotton cloth, and refold it along new lines every few months so the zari does not crack on the creases.

    How long does delivery take?

    Most orders arrive in 4 to 6 days. Bridal pieces and made-to-order blouses take longer, and we confirm the date on a call after you order.

    Can I exchange or return a saree?

    Unworn sarees with tags can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Custom-stitched blouses and jewellery are final sale.

    Do you stitch blouses?

    Blouses are sold in sizes 34 to 42. For a custom fit, choose the matching blouse and mention your measurements on the order call.

    Which payment methods are accepted?

    UPI, cards and cash on delivery are listed at checkout. This demo store does not take real payments.
